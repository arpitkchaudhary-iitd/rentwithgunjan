# Customer Data and Future Marketing Plan

## Goal
Build a customer database that can support repeat rentals, future Tesla Taxi / self-driving scheduled rides, and later cross-sell opportunities for real estate services.

## Customer data to collect
The following customer information should be stored securely:
- Full name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Date of birth (if required for rental compliance)
- Driver license verification status
- Preferred city / pickup location
- Booking history
- Estimated repeat rental frequency
- Marketing opt-in status
- Preferred communication channel
- Notes for customer service and loyalty programs

## Future product opportunities
1. Repeat customer loyalty program
2. Tesla Taxi / self-driving scheduled ride service
3. Premium membership for frequent renters
4. Referral rewards
5. Bundle offers for local transportation and real estate services

## Marketing plan for future products
- Collect consent for email and SMS marketing
- Track repeat rental behavior to identify high-value customers
- Segment customers by:
  - frequent renter
  - premium customer
  - local Hoboken / Jersey City resident
  - business travel customer
- Use booking history to offer future services such as:
  - Tesla taxi
  - scheduled ride memberships
  - local real estate referrals or property-related offers

## Real estate cross-sell opportunity
Since Gunjan is a real estate agent in New Jersey, the system can safely collect optional profile information such as:
- interest in buying or renting property
- preferred neighborhood
- property type interest
- household status
- contact preference for future real estate offers

This data should be stored only with explicit consent and used for future marketing campaigns, not for immediate rental operations.

## Data privacy and compliance notes
- Obtain explicit consent for marketing communication
- Store customer data securely in the database
- Limit access to marketing and admin roles only
- Provide a clear opt-out mechanism for future campaigns
- Follow applicable privacy laws for New Jersey and any future expansion states

## Suggested future features
- Customer profile dashboard
- Repeat-customer loyalty score
- Marketing preference center
- CRM-style notes and tags
- Cross-sell campaign management
